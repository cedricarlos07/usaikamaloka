Mobile Money Fraud in Africa

Protect your transfers and accounts from digital fraud.

Mobile money is the backbone of Africa's digital economy. Fraudsters exploit it through fake agents, prize scams, account-lock links and psychological pressure via calls or WhatsApp.

Common warning signs

  • Requests for PIN or OTP via message
  • Instant prize promises requiring a 'fee'
  • Unknown numbers posing as official support
  • Short links to fake login pages
  • Artificial urgency ('your account will be blocked')

Best practices

  • Never share PIN or OTP
  • Verify numbers through official channels
  • Use FOTIP CI to analyze suspicious links or numbers
  • Report scams to operators and authorities
