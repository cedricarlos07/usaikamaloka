Kamaloka AI Technologies

Technology lab and venture studio. Born in Africa. Built for the world.

Who we are

Kamaloka AI Technologies LLC is an Ivorian technology company that designs, builds and launches high-impact digital products. We combine artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, software engineering and automation to solve real problems — in Côte d'Ivoire, across Africa and internationally.

Our model

We are not a traditional services agency. Kamaloka operates as a lab and venture studio: we identify underserved markets, build the technology, launch the product and accelerate when the market responds.

  • Observe real-world problems on the ground
  • Reimagine solutions with AI
  • Build and ship fast
  • Measure, learn, accelerate

Where we operate

Operational headquarters in Bingerville / Abidjan (Côte d'Ivoire), with a US presence in Albuquerque, NM. This dual footprint lets us build for African realities while meeting global standards.
